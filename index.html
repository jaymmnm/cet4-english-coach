PASSAGE 01 · 5 MINUTES

Learning in small blocks

Many students believe that they need long hours to learn English well. In fact, short and regular study sessions can be more helpful. When learners study for thirty to sixty minutes, they are often able to stay focused. They can review a few words, read one short passage, and listen to a simple recording.

The key is to return to English every day. A notebook can help learners notice their progress. After several weeks, small efforts become a strong habit.
